Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. Pullup Trainer has no account, no server, and no analytics. It makes no network requests. Your tests, sessions, preferences, and band settings are stored only in the app's local database on your iPhone and are deleted when you delete the app or use “Reset all data” in Settings.

Apple Health

If you turn on “Save to Apple Health”, the app writes each finished session to Apple Health as a strength-training workout and reads your most recent body weight to estimate energy burned. This happens on your device through Apple's HealthKit framework. The app never sends Health data anywhere, and you can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

Notifications

Session reminders and rest-timer alerts are scheduled locally on your phone. No push notification service is used.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
