How it works

Do a test: as many strict pullups as you can. Your score picks a training cycle of six or nine sessions, five sets each, with 120 seconds of rest between sets. Finish the cycle, rest two days, test again, get the next cycle.

The app enforces the rest days. It tells you when the next session unlocks and won't let you train early.

Privacy

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no server, no analytics. Apple Health is optional. Read the privacy policy.
